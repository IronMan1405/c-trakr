# C-Trakr

**The Fitbit for vehicles.**  

C-Trakr is an application that tracks the carbon emissions generated each time a vehicle is used.

It helps users understand their environmental impact, improve driving habits, and make cleaner mobility choices over time.

## Why C-Trakr?

Private vehicle emissions are a major contributor to urban pollution and climate impact.

Most drivers track fuel costs; but not environmental cost.

C-Trakr bridges that gap by giving users real-time insights into:

- CO₂ emissions per trip
- Driving efficiency
- Fuel waste patterns
- Long-term environmental impact

## Key Features

- Daily / Weekly / Monthly emission analytics
- Trip-based carbon tracking
- Fuel efficiency insights
- Eco-driving score
- Historical trends & smart graphs
- Live vehicle data integration (in progress)

## Vision

C-Trakr aims to become a consumer-friendly sustainability platform for commuters, fleet owners, and future vehicle manufacturers.

Helping users answer one simple question:

**How much is my driving costing the planet?**
